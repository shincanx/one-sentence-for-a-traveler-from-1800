A deterministic Python program that writes one sentence — at most 200 characters — explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800, to /output/entry.txt. Every word in it predates 1800: a letter that talks back, copper wires, lightning, a parlour, books.

> A letter that talks back, racing along copper wires swift as lightning, so the whole world may sit in your parlour, speak, and open its books to you.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
